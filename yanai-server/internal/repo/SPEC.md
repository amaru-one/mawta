## Purpose
All hand-written SQL behind the HTTP handlers and job enqueueing: one file per domain area, each function taking a `pgx.Tx` already carrying tenant context.

## Contents
- `auth.go` — pre-tenant (via `db.WithAuthTx`): `FindLoginCandidate`, `ResolveMembership` (the user's one non-revoked membership — `memberships_one_school_per_user` means there is never more than one to choose from), `RecordLoginFailure`/`RecordLoginSuccess`, `CreateSession`, `FindLiveSession`, `TouchSession` (throttled write), `RevokeSession`/`RevokeSessionByTokenHash`/`RevokeUserSessions`, `PruneSessions` (opportunistic, called on login — see Conventions). Post-tenant (via `db.WithTenantTx`): `ResolveTeacher`, the surviving half of the old `tenantAuth` query.
- `home.go` — `TenantToday`, `GetHome`. `GetHome` answers with the teacher's day and week as **dated sessions**: `Today` is every `class_sessions` row for the date (with `room` and `topic`), `Week` is the rest of the ISO week grouped into `HomeWeekDay`s. Neither filters `status` — home reports the day as it stands, cancelled and finished sessions included, and the client renders those states rather than hiding them. `me.go` — `GetMe`. `profile.go` — `GetProfile`.
- `classes.go` — `GetClassDashboard` (`Distribution` counts *asserted* niveles de logro from `competency_term_levels`, not computed grades; unexported helpers `getClassInfo`, `listSessions`). The `next_session` / `upcoming` / `past` split is by wall clock, not by `session_date` alone: `next_session` is today's earliest session with `scheduled_end_at >= now()` (and status scheduled/in_progress), `past` also takes today's sessions whose `scheduled_end_at < now()`, `upcoming` also takes today's still-unfinished sessions — so a class that meets twice in a day surfaces the current/next one and files the finished one under "anteriores". The caller strips the `next_session` row back out of `upcoming`.
- `sessions.go` — `GetSessionHeader` (incl. the section-average level), `GetSessionRoster` (attendance, voice notes — no observation, flag, level, trend or participation surface any more). `roster.go` — `RosterAsOf` (enrollment as of a date).
- `attendance.go` — `GetSessionMeta`, `UpsertAttendance`, `MarkAllPresent`, `MaybeStampAttendanceTaken`.
- `grades.go` — `GetStudentGrades`, `GetEffectiveScheme`, `GetComponentIDByCode`, `ListGradingComponents`, `CreateAssessment` (takes a `NewAssessment`; optional `StudentID` scopes the evidence to one student, optional `CriterionIDs` links it to criterios, `CountsTowardInternal` keeps it in or out of the school's own average), unexported `linkAssessmentCriteria`, `DeleteAssessment` (soft; returns a `DeletedAssessment` so the caller knows who to recompute), `CreateScore`, `CorrectScore` (supersedes the old score row; correcting twice concurrently 409s), `CreateCriterionScore`, `CorrectCriterionScore` (same supersede protocol, scoped to one criterio within an assessment), unexported `resolveCriterionValue` (resolves a `raw_value` or a `level`, never both, into the `criterion_scores` row to write), `requireMaxScoreForNumeric` (a numeric criterion score needs the assessment to carry a `max_score`), `requireAssessmentCriterionTag` (a criterio can only be scored if the assessment was tagged with it via `assessment_criteria`). `EvaluationEntry` carries no weight, reports `IsPersonal`, `CountsTowardInternal`, `ValueScale` and a nullable `MaxScore`, lists the criterios the evidence is **tagged with** — text only, no level — and carries `CriterionScores`, the independent per-criterio marks; `ScoreAttempt` carries a numeric `RawValue` only, the assessment's own overall mark. `CriterionScoreEntry` carries `ValueScale`, `RawValue`, `MaxValue` and `Level`, whichever of the last two the criterio was scored on. `ComputedGradeView` is the optional internal weighted system and carries no projection. The response carries no AI note summary — nothing writes a `note_summaries` row.
- `competencies.go` — `ListClassCompetencyLevels` (levels + conclusion state, with the mandatory-conclusion classification), `AssertCompetencyLevel` and `UpsertConclusion` (both append-only with supersession), `conclusionRequirement`, and the shared `classifyConclusionRequirement(inclusionSupport, stage, cycle, levelCode, consecutiveC)` both call into: inclusion support is mandatory every period; Inicial Ciclo I requires a conclusión for every competencia regardless of level; otherwise the level itself is mandatory or not by the student's nivel educativo (level C in Secundaria/Primaria/Inicial Ciclo II, level B in Primaria/Inicial Ciclo II, level A only in Inicial Ciclo II), and level C carries the stronger `consecutive_c` classification when the competencia was also C in the immediately preceding period.
- `criteria.go` — the criterios de evaluación layer, **authoring only**: `CapabilityRow`, `ListCompetencyCapabilities`, `CreateCapability` (the capacidades of a competencia, the picker a teacher writes a criterio against), `ListClassCriteria` (every competencia of the class's área with its criterios; not scoped to a student, because nothing here is per-student), `CreateCriterion` (rejects a blank description, a competencia outside the class's área, and a capacidad outside the competencia), `DeleteCriterion` (soft). `CriterionRow` carries a `CapabilityID` and no level, and must not grow one — a criterio is defined, not graded. Marks against a criterio are per-evidence and live in `criterion_scores` (see `grades.go`), never on the criterio itself.
- `students.go` — the two reads the evaluating screens are built on: `ListClassStudents` (the roster as of today with each student's level in one competencia and how many pieces of evidencia stand behind it) and `ListStudentEvidence` (that student's evidence for the period, narrowed to the competencia **through the criterio tags**, each piece carrying its own value on its own scale). Both reuse the canonical roster/assessment joins rather than inventing new ones; neither aggregates anything.
- `practice_scores.go` — create/delete/list unofficial practice scores.
- `voicenotes.go` — `CreateVoiceNote` (audio bytes in DB), `GetVoiceNoteFile`, `VoiceNoteExists` (used by the save confirmation — there is no state left for save to write), `DeleteVoiceNote` (soft; discard), `SetVoiceNoteTranscript` (completed), `MarkVoiceNoteTranscriptionFailed`.
- `notifications.go` — list (cursor on created_at), mark read/read-all.
- `terms.go` — `ResolveTerm` (explicit id or current term).
- `events.go` — `LogEvent`, `LogStudentReads` (mandatory for reads returning identifiable students).
- `jobs.go` — `EnqueueJob` (queue hardcoded 'default'; dedupes on `(kind, unique_key)` while available/retryable/scheduled/running).

## Inputs / Outputs
Inputs: `pgx.Tx` from `db.WithTenantTx` (or worker transactions) — RLS scopes every query. Outputs: response structs serialized by `httpapi`, plus rows in `jobs` and `events`.

## Conventions & Constraints
- Functions never open transactions; they run inside the caller's.
- Tenant id on inserts comes from `app.current_tenant_id()`, never a Go parameter (except `EnqueueJob`, whose tenant_id column is outside RLS, and `auth.CreateSession`, whose `user_sessions.tenant_id` comes from the just-resolved membership — the row exists precisely because no tenant context exists yet at that point in the login flow).
- **Pruning `user_sessions` is opportunistic, not a job.** `internal/worker/SPEC.md` records that there is no ticker or scheduler in this codebase, so a `session.prune` job kind would have no enqueuer — this repo has already been burned once by a job kind with no enqueuer. Expired/idle sessions are revoked lazily, at validation time in `internal/httpapi.sessionAuth`; `repo.PruneSessions` hard-deletes long-revoked/expired rows and is called best-effort at the end of a successful login.
- Scores and computed grades are append-only: corrections insert + stamp `superseded_at`; no in-place updates.
- Soft deletes (`deleted_at`) everywhere; filter them in every query.

## Conventions & Constraints addendum
- A competency with no level for a period is reported as null, never as C: "no desarrollada" and "en inicio" are different facts (RVM 048-2024, 5.1.2.2 p.1).
- **Nothing derives a nivel de logro.** `AssertCompetencyLevel` leaves `suggested_level_id` and `computed_grade_id` null on purpose: under RVM 094-2020 the level is the teacher's judgement of the period's evidence against the criterios, and a computed number sitting beside it in the UI is exactly the framing the norm moves away from. Criterio levels are evidence and are never rolled up.
- **A criterio never receives a nivel de logro, and this package has no function that would.** A criterio says what to look at in a piece of work — it is the ruler, not the official judgement (RVM 094-2020, 5.1.1.3 p.3). It *tags* a piece of evidencia (`assessment_criteria`), and that tag is what lets `ListStudentEvidence` group evidence under the competencia it informs. The one recorded judgement of a competencia is `AssertCompetencyLevel`, writing `competency_term_levels`.
  This prohibition is about the official level, not about marking evidence. A criterio **does** carry a per-evidence mark inside one assessment, written by `CreateCriterionScore`/`CorrectCriterionScore` into `criterion_scores` — numeric (`raw_value`/`max_value`) or ordinal (`scale_level_id`, the same AD/A/B/C ladder). That mark is evidence and is never rolled up: see "Nothing derives a nivel de logro" above. Read as a blanket ban, the earlier wording contradicted this file's own Contents section, the routes in `internal/httpapi`, and the `criterion_scores` DDL — and, under the spec-wins rule in `AGENTS.md`, instructed a reader to delete working, constraint-enforced behaviour. What may never be added is a route or function that writes a *nivel de logro* for a criterio.
- **`scores` carries a numeric mark only.** `CorrectScore` inherits the old `raw_value` when a correction names none, replaces it when it names one, and clears it entirely for any status but `graded` — a `not_submitted` row must not keep a number on it. A per-criterio level lives in `criterion_scores`, never in `scores`.
- **A criterion score carries a mark or a level, never both.** `resolveCriterionValue` rejects a body with both (`two_values`) and resolves a level against the ordinal `grade_scales`; `requireMaxScoreForNumeric` and `requireAssessmentCriterionTag` gate the write before it reaches SQL. `CorrectCriterionScore` re-validates both on the replacement row, since a correction can move a criterion score between assessments' constraints.
- **A rubric assessment (`value_scale = 'rubric'`) never gets a `scores` row and never counts toward the school's internal average.** It is valued only through `criterion_scores`; `counts_toward_internal` is forced false for it at the HTTP layer (see `internal/httpapi/SPEC.md`) and the DB itself rejects the combination.
- **The school's own average is the only consumer of `counts_toward_internal`.** No criterio or competency query filters on it: the official judgement counts every piece of evidence regardless.
- `competency_term_levels.class_id` is provenance, not identity. Scope reads by the class's *área* and roster, so a level asserted from another class still counts for its own área and never counts for someone else's.
- **`classes.go`'s dashboard "pendientes" queries also consult `criterion_scores`.** A rubric assessment carries no `scores` row at all, so without this it could never be marked done on the dashboard.
