## Purpose
The complete database schema as goose SQL migrations, applied in order against PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector.

## Contents
- `00001_initial_schema.sql` — the complete schema in one migration: every table, view, function, trigger and RLS policy, in final form. Squashed from an 18-file incremental history (`00001_bootstrap.sql` through `00018_competency_levels.sql`) on 2026-08-26 — see Change Log. Internally organised in the same order the original files were, as section-comment banners, so `git blame`/history intuition about "which era added this" still roughly holds even though the file boundaries are gone.
- `00002_evaluation_criteria.sql` — adds the CNEB layer below `competencies`: `capabilities` (capacidades), `performance_standards` (desempeños, per capacidad × grado), `evaluation_criteria` (criterios de evaluación — what a teacher writes/loads), `assessment_criteria` (join to assessments, sibling of `assessment_competencies`), and `evaluation_criterion_scores` (append-only per-student rubric results, on the tenant's existing AD/A/B/C scale). See Change Log.
- `00003_criterion_term_levels.sql` — makes evaluation por criterios workable under RVM 094-2020: `evaluation_criterion_scores.assessment_id` becomes nullable so a criterio level can be the teacher's judgement for a whole periodo rather than only a per-quiz cell; drops `computed_grades`' projection columns; drops the entire predictive subsystem (`risk_rules`, `risk_flags`, `risk_flag_evidence`, `notifications.risk_flag_id`). See Change Log.
- `ANALYSIS.md` — design notes: verified up/down/up cleanliness, expected-painful queries, ambiguities in the HTML spec, argued constraints. Predates the squash and `00002`; still valid as design record, though its object counts (49 tables, 350 indexes, ...) are a snapshot from before `00016`/`00018`/`00002` and are superseded by the live counts against the current migration set.

## Inputs / Outputs
Applied with goose (`GOOSE_MIGRATION_DIR` points here in production). Downstream consumers: every Go package, `seed/seed_demo_tenant.sql`.

## Conventions & Constraints
- Migrations are schema only — seed/demo data lives in `seed/`, never here.
- Append-only, never edit an applied migration; add a new numbered file (`00002_...` and up — `00001` is the squashed baseline, not a precedent for editing in place).
- Text + CHECK "enums" in SQL are mirrored as constants in `internal/domain/consts.go` (cascade when adding values).
- Every tenant table gets FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY; `jobs` deliberately has no RLS (nullable tenant_id, workers span tenants).
- Column/table `comment on` strings are load-bearing documentation — keep them current.

## Change Log
- 2026-08-27: Added `00003_criterion_term_levels.sql`. RVM 094-2020 (mod. RVM 048-2024) determines achievement by the teacher's judgement of evidence against **criterios de evaluación**, one set per competencia — no weighted average anywhere, and no nivel de logro at área level. `00002` built the criterio layer at the wrong grain for that: `assessment_id` was `not null` and `ecs_live_key` unique on `(assessment_id, criterion_id, student_id)`, so a criterio level could only exist as a per-quiz cell, whereas the norm judges a criterio over the whole periodo. `assessment_id` is now nullable, with two live keys — `ecs_live_assessment_key` for the evidence-from-one-assessment shape and `ecs_live_term_key` (`criterion_id, student_id, term_id`) for the period judgement — plus `ecs_scope_supplied_without_assessment` requiring the caller to supply `class_id`/`term_id` when there is no assessment to copy them from. `app.sync_criterion_score_denormalised` was rewritten to branch on that, keeping the closed-term guard on both paths and the draft-assessment guard on the assessment path. Also dropped `computed_grades.projected_min_normalized`/`projected_max_normalized` (the forecast) and the whole predictive subsystem — `risk_rules`, `risk_flags`, `risk_flag_evidence`, and `notifications.risk_flag_id` with its `notifications_alert_has_evidence` check. `computed_grades` itself survives, re-documented as the school's optional internal weighted system (D.S. 009-2006-ED art. 37.4.f), never the official record. Deliberately kept without a writer: `competency_year_snapshots` and `year_final_situations`, which are the official year-end acta records the norm requires (5.1.3), not forecasts. Verified up/down/up clean; the period-grain insert, its uniqueness, and the closed-term guard exercised against the seed tenant.
- 2026-08-26: Added `00002_evaluation_criteria.sql`. The schema had `competencies` (área-level) and rolled scores straight up to `competency_term_levels`, but nothing between them: the CNEB chain is Área curricular -> Competencia -> Capacidades (fixed, national, 2-4 per competencia) -> Desempeños (national, per grado/ciclo) -> the teacher's own criterios de evaluación (desempeños precisados). That last layer — what a teacher actually writes and loads to evaluate a competencia — had no table. Added `capabilities`, `performance_standards`, `evaluation_criteria`, `assessment_criteria` (sibling of `assessment_competencies`, at criterio granularity) and `evaluation_criterion_scores` (append-only per-student rubric cell, reusing `grade_scale_levels` rather than a second rubric vocabulary, mirroring `scores`' denormalisation and append-only triggers). Deliberately out of scope: `competency_term_levels` is untouched — it stays a teacher's judgement call, never computed from criterio scores, matching how `computed_grades.suggested_level_id` already works for competencies. Verified up/down/up clean; full-chain insert, closed-term guard, live-row uniqueness, cross-tenant RLS rejection and the append-only mutation guard all exercised manually against the seed tenant and rolled back with zero residue. `privacy.undeclared_student_references` still returns only the pre-existing `practice_scores` gap — `evaluation_criterion_scores` carries the required `MINORS' PERSONAL DATA` marker.
- 2026-08-26: Squashed the 18-file incremental history (`00001_bootstrap.sql` .. `00018_competency_levels.sql`) into `00001_initial_schema.sql`, after the local database was dropped and before any environment depended on the intermediate steps. No behavioural change — every table is in the same final form the chain produced. Four spots carried genuine historical churn and are written directly in final form rather than replayed as a later ALTER: `competencies.subject_id` (created `not null`, `00004`; made nullable + `kind` added, `00018`), `risk_flag_evidence`'s `entity_type` CHECK (6 values, `00012`; redefined to 7, `00018`), `voice_notes.storage_key` (created `not null`, `00011`; made nullable + `audio bytea` added, `00015`), and `jobs`' table/`kind` comments (written in `00013`, rewritten in `00017` and again in `00018`). Reproduced as-is, not fixed by the squash: `privacy.anonymize_student()` clears `voice_notes.transcript`/`embedding` but never clears `audio` (added after the function was last touched) — a pre-existing gap, worth its own migration later. Verified: `up`/`down`/`up` clean; catalog counts (55 base tables incl. `goose_db_version`, 53 RLS-forced, 20 `app.*` + 1 `privacy.*` function, the `tenants` ↔ `grading_schemes` FK cycle) match the pre-squash chain.
- 2026-08-26: Added `00018_competency_levels.sql`. The schema previously had no place for a nivel de logro per competencia — the thing RVM 094-2020 actually regulates — so `computed_grades` was standing in for it, which the norm forbids ("no se asignará un nivel de logro a cada área curricular", 5.1.1.3 p.12). The new tables make the teacher's assertion the record and demote the computation to `suggested_level_id`. `competencies.subject_id` became nullable because the two CNEB competencias transversales are adscribed to no área; `competencies_subject_code_key` is partial and stops covering them, hence `competencies_transversal_code_key`. Verified up/down/up clean.
- 2026-08-25: Added `00017_worker_job_kinds.sql` after the worker was reduced to two job kinds. `jobs.kind` is plain text with no CHECK, so this is a documentation-only migration; rows of retired kinds are left to fail as 'unknown job kind' rather than being rewritten.
- 2026-08-24: Initial spec created retroactively, describing current contents.

## Open Questions
- `competency_year_snapshots` and `year_final_situations` have no writer since `00003` removed `internal/promotion`. The tables are correct and normative (the situación final of 5.1.3); the code that filled them was cut as part of removing everything predictive. Whoever restores year-end close should write to them rather than inventing new tables.
- `evaluation_criteria.performance_standard_id`'s capacidad is not checked against `evaluation_criteria.competency_id` — nothing stops linking a criterio to a desempeño that belongs to a different competencia. Every other cross-table consistency this strict in the schema is enforced by a trigger (e.g. `app.sync_score_denormalised`); this one is a known, deliberate gap pending a decision on whether it's worth one.

## Resolved
- *Is MINEDU's "B — En proceso" a pass?* (was: `passing_value` and `is_passing` disagreeing in the seed). The question was mis-posed. Under RVM 048-2024 "aprobado" is not a property of a level at all: an área counts as approved only "si cumple las condiciones establecidas en la tabla que dispone las condiciones de promoción o permanencia" (Anexo 09), and that table makes B sufficient in 1.°/3.°/4.° and insufficient in 2.°/5.°. The cutoff both columns encoded — nota 11 — comes from Anexo 07, which governed the 2020 school year only and expired when transitoria 8.3 extended the literal scale to all of Secundaria on 1 March 2021. `00018` therefore documents both columns as advisory, and `internal/promotion` decides promotion by counting levels per área.
