## Purpose
Student grades screen. Leads with the official MINEDU record — the nivel de logro per competencia, judged against the criterios de evaluación — then the evidencia, where each piece carries both its mark and its level per criterio, then the school's optional internal weighted grade, collapsed.

This screen is where "one capture, two readings" lives: the teacher opens a piece of work once and files the mark (the school's number) and the criterio levels (the official judgement) side by side. What splits is where each one goes, never the teacher's effort.

## Contents
- `+page.svelte` — loads `getStudentGrades` + `getStudentCompetencies` + `getClassCriteria` + `getGradingComponents` (`Promise.all`), owns local `$state` for all four (this screen predates and does not follow the `mappers.ts`-only convention for `StudentGradesDto`; the competencias code does, via `toCompetencyLevel`; criterios and components are consumed as DTOs). Renders, in order: "NIVELES DE LOGRO POR COMPETENCIA" (a `CompetencyCard` per competency, each holding that competencia's criterios), "EVIDENCIA" (the evaluation-module accordions plus "Añadir evidencia"), the collapsed "Sistema interno de la IE" card, and "NOTAS PERSONALES" with "Añadir nota personal".

## Inputs / Outputs
Route param `studentId`; `classId`/`studentName`/`courseName` travel as query params (`?class=&name=&course=`) since no endpoint returns them and there is no other student lookup. Mutations: `createScore`/`correctScore` (with retry-on-`already_superseded`), `createAssessment`/`deleteAssessment` from `lib/api/endpoints/classes.ts`, `createPracticeScore`/`deletePracticeScore`, `assertCompetencyLevel`/`upsertConclusion`, and `assertCriterionLevel` from `lib/api/endpoints/criteria.ts` (which now always names the evidencia the level was read from). Reads `usesInternalGrading` from `lib/stores.ts`, loaded once by the root layout from `GET /me`.

## Conventions & Constraints
- **Order is the argument.** Competencias first, marks second, weighted grade last and collapsed. RVM 094-2020 determines achievement from the teacher's judgement against the criterios; a layout that led with a computed number would contradict the norm no matter what the copy said.
- **Nothing on this screen derives a level.** Criterio levels are evidence and are never summed, averaged or rolled up into the competency level; the competency card says so in plain Spanish under its picker. There is no suggested level anywhere.
- **A criterio level is recorded inside its evidencia, never on the criterio row.** Expanding a piece of evidence reveals `EvidenceCriteriaPickers` for the criterios it declares; the competency card's criterios are a read-only timeline (`CriterionTimeline`) whose cells link back to the evidence they came from. Until 2026-08-28 the criterio row itself carried a picker, which had the teacher grading the ruler instead of the work (RVM 094-2020, 5.1.1.3 p.3).
- **The school's internal system is optional.** With `usesInternalGrading` false the mark inputs and the "Sistema interno de la IE" card disappear entirely and the criterio pickers carry on unchanged — the official record never came from a number in the first place. Evidence filed out of the average shows a "Fuera del promedio interno" tag rather than being hidden; it is still evidencia.
- **Evidence added here is for this student alone.** Every `createAssessment` from this screen sends `student_id`; class-wide evidence is filed from the class dashboard, which speaks for the class rather than for one student. The dialog says so in its caption and the row carries a "Solo este alumno" badge, because an assessment that quietly appeared on thirty other screens would be indistinguishable from one that did not.
- The accordion groups by **grading component**, not by kind. The kind (Quiz, Tarea, Proyecto…) rides as a label on the row instead: the component is what the school's scheme is built from, whereas the kind is descriptive. A row's kind and its section can therefore legitimately disagree.
- Deleting evidence is soft and the confirm dialog says what survives: the notes stay in the student's history but stop being shown and stop counting in the internal weighted grade. There is no edit — same rule as criterios, for the same reason.
- "Añadir nota personal" is a _different_ thing from "Añadir evidencia" and must stay visibly so. A nota personal never reaches the record; evidencia does. Until 2026-08-28 the only button on this screen said "Añadir evaluación" and created a nota personal, which is exactly the confusion the two labels now prevent.
- Marks carry **no weight**. `EvaluationDto` has no `weight` field and the module headers show only "N/M evaluadas". The standing caption under "EVIDENCIA" states that marks do not determine the nivel de logro — that sentence is load-bearing, not decoration.
- The "Sistema interno de la IE" card is the weighted `computed_grade`, collapsed by default and labelled as the school's own system rather than the MINEDU record (D.S. 009-2006-ED art. 37.4.f). It carries no projection: the norm has no forecast grade, and the columns were dropped in `00003`.
- A competency or criterio with `level === null` means "not evaluated this period" (RVM 048-2024, 5.1.2.2 p.1) — rendered blank/dash, never as C.
- Asserting a level (competency or criterio) is autosave-instant: tap = saved, matching the rest of the app. Each save is a deliberate per-tap write, not a background sync — see `CompetencyCard`'s doc comment.
- Long CNEB competency and criterio texts are full sentences: every row follows the app's overflow idiom — `.grow{flex:1;min-width:0}` on the text, no ellipsis, `flex-shrink:0` on badges/icons, `align-items:flex-start` on rows whose text can wrap.
- A `final` conclusión descriptiva without the "incluye recomendaciones" checkbox is blocked client-side in `CompetencyCard`, mirroring the server's 422 `missing_recommendations` — the server is still the source of truth.

- 2026-08-28: Evidencia × criterio. Each evidence row now expands to a per-criterio `LevelPicker` block, and `handleCriterionLevelChange` sends the `assessment_id` — the criterio row in the competency card lost its picker and became `CriterionTimeline`, whose cells call `openEvidence` to expand and scroll to the work they came from. The mark input, the internal-grade card and the dialog's "sobre" field are now gated on `usesInternalGrading`; a row whose evidence is out of the school's average is tagged rather than hidden. The evidence caption states the two-readings rule, and an evidence with no criterios says so instead of listing an empty set. Cascade: `lib/components/`, `lib/api/`, `lib/stores.ts`, new route `class/[courseId]/evidence/[assessmentId]/`.
- 2026-08-28: Added "Añadir evidencia" (`AddEvidenceDialog`, scope `student`) at the foot of the EVIDENCIA section and a per-row delete with confirmation; the newly created evidence's accordion is expanded on return so the mark can be typed straight away. The old "Añadir evaluación" button was renamed "Añadir nota personal" — it always created a `practice_score`, and its label was the reason evidence looked impossible to add. `criteriaByCompetency` became a `$derived` over the grouped `criteriaGroups`, which the dialog needs. Cascade: `lib/components/`, `lib/api/`, `routes/class/[courseId]/siagie-report/`.

Deleted the "RESULTADO PARCIAL" hero, the "X% del curso evaluado · falta Y%" bar and the "Rango posible al cierre" projection card; moved competencias to the top and gave each card its criterios with a `LevelPicker` apiece; renamed "EVALUACIONES" to "EVIDENCIA", dropped "Peso NN%" from every module header, dropped the per-assessment achievement percentage, and added the criterios each assessment evidences; demoted the weighted grade to a collapsed, explicitly-labelled internal-system card.
